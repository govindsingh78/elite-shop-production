## Full description (4,000 characters max)

Elitezaar is online shopping for everyday style: hand-picked clothing and accessories for women, men and kids, from independent sellers you'll love.

WHY SHOP ON ELITEZAAR
• Every listing reviewed — our team checks each seller's product before it appears in the store.
• Pay your way — UPI and net banking through Razorpay, or cash on delivery.
• Live order tracking — follow your parcel from packing to your door.
• Easy returns — 7-day returns on eligible items, and refunds in 5–7 business days.
• Real help — call or email our customer care team whenever you need us.

SHOP THE WAY YOU LIKE
• Browse collections and subcategories, from wardrobe staples to the finishing touches.
• Search by name, filter by brand and price, and sort by what's new or the best price.
• Save favourites to your wishlist and come back to them on any device.
• Read honest ratings and reviews from customers who bought the product.
• Check out as a guest, or create an account to save addresses for faster checkout.
• Sign in with Google in one tap.

FOR SELLERS
Have something worth selling? Open your Elitezaar store from the app: list products with photos, manage stock, and ship and track orders from your seller hub.

Elitezaar is founded by Govind Singh with a simple idea: shopping online should feel as personal as walking into a good local shop, where someone has chosen every piece with care.
